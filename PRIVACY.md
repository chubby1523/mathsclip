# MathsClip privacy policy

**Last updated:** 24 November 2026

MathsClip doesn't collect, store, send, sell or share any personal information. That includes your browsing history, the pages you visit, and what you copy.

## What happens to your data

- **Everything runs in your browser.** Finding equations, converting them to LaTeX and writing to your clipboard all happen on your device. MathsClip makes no network requests and uses no analytics.
- **Your settings are saved with Chrome Sync.** MathsClip uses `chrome.storage.sync` to save your choices, such as the delimiter style, Markdown mode and Click-to-Copy. If you're signed in to Chrome with sync on, Google syncs these settings between your browsers, as it does for any extension. Nothing else is stored: no page content and no copied equations.

## Permissions

- **Right-click menu (`contextMenus`):** adds "Copy Selection with LaTeX" and "Copy Equation as LaTeX" to the right-click menu.
- **Storage (`storage`):** saves your settings, as described above.
- **Clipboard (`clipboardWrite`):** lets the right-click menu and Click-to-Copy put LaTeX on your clipboard.
- **Access to all websites:** maths can appear on any page, so MathsClip needs to run everywhere to find it. It looks for equations on the page so they're ready when you copy, and nothing it reads leaves your browser.

## Third parties

None. The only outside service involved is Chrome Sync, which Chrome itself provides, for your settings.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling MathsClip removes its saved settings.
